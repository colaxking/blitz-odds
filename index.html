How Blitz Edge works
  • What the number is. The model's estimate of each team's chance to win the game outright. The two percentages add up to 100.
  • What goes in. Each team's league ranks in total, rushing and passing yards on offense and defense, home field, players listed out or questionable, and the forecast for outdoor games. Early in the season, last year's final ranks are blended in.
  • When it updates. It recalculates as team stats, injury reports, and weather change, up to kickoff. A finished game keeps showing the pregame call.
  • What it isn't. A guarantee, or betting advice. It is an estimate.

How to read these cards
  • Model is the Blitz Edge pregame win probability for its pick.
  • Market is the win probability the posted moneylines imply for the same team, with the sportsbook's margin removed.
  • The big number is model minus market, in percentage points. A wide gap means the two estimates disagree. It is a reason to look closer at a game, not a betting edge.
  • A high win probability is not a gap. A 90% favorite the market also has at 90% shows 0.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an NFL pick'em league?

A pick'em league is a pool where everyone picks the winners of each week's NFL games. Picks are scored after the games finish, and the standings rank everyone across the season.

Is Blitz Odds free to use?

Yes. Creating a league, joining a league, and making picks are free.

How do confidence pools work?

You rank your picks from most to least confident. A correct pick earns the points you assigned to it, so your surest calls are worth the most.

Can I create a private pick'em league?

Yes. A league can be public, private, or hidden. Private and hidden leagues are joined by invitation or code.

How does automatic scoring work?

Picks lock at kickoff. When a game goes final, Blitz Odds grades every pick and updates the league standings.

What is Blitz Edge?

Blitz Edge is the Blitz Odds prediction model. It estimates each NFL team's chance to win from offense and defense rankings, injuries, rest, and weather. It is an estimate, not a guarantee.

Does Blitz Odds show live NFL scores?

Yes. Scores, quarter, and game clock update while games are in progress, next to the model's pregame pick.

Can I view college football scores and predictions?

Yes. College football scores are shown with win probabilities based on the betting market. The Blitz Edge model currently covers the NFL.
