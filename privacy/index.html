Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Blitz Odds is a free NFL pick'em and matchup analysis tool. This page explains what information the site collects, why, and what you can do about it. We do not sell personal information, and we do not run third-party advertising.

Information collected automatically

Every visit is recorded by our own analytics, which is designed to measure traffic without identifying you personally. Each pageview or interaction records:

  • The page or section viewed, the path, and the site host
  • The referring website's domain only - never the full referring URL
  • A general device category (mobile, tablet, or desktop)
  • Approximate location - city, region, and country - derived by our hosting provider from the network connection. Your IP address is not stored.
  • Your display preferences at the time of the visit (theme, timezone, selected sportsbook)
  • A random visitor ID stored in your browser, used to tell repeat visits from new ones. It is not tied to your name or email and is cleared if you clear your browser storage.

We also use Google Analytics, which sets its own cookies and is governed by Google's privacy policy. You can opt out using Google's browser add-on.

Information you provide

Most of the site works without an account. If you create one, we store:

  • Account details - your email address and password, handled by our authentication provider. We never see or store your password.
  • Profile - a display name and a chosen avatar from a fixed set. Your display name is visible to other members of any league you join.
  • League activity - leagues you create or join, the picks you submit, and your standings. Picks and standings are visible to other members of that league.
  • Preferences - favorite teams, theme, timezone, and sportsbook selection, so they follow you across devices.

Email and notifications

We send email only for things you or a league you belong to initiate - league invitations, join requests, and account messages. These are sent through our email provider, Resend. We do not send marketing email.

If you follow an individual game to get alerts on it, we store that game’s identifier against your account for that week only. It is used solely to decide whether to send you an alert about that game, is never shared with anyone, and is automatically cleared a few weeks after the week it belongs to.

If you turn on push notifications, your browser gives us an anonymous subscription token for that device so we can deliver kickoff reminders, score updates, and injury alerts. The token identifies the browser, not you. You can revoke it at any time in the site's notification settings or in your browser's site permissions. Alongside the token we store a short label describing the device (for example “Safari on iOS”) so you can tell your registered devices apart, and the time of the last successful delivery to it.

We also keep a short-lived record of what happened to each alert - which alert it was, which account it was for, and the outcome, including the reasons an alert was deliberately not sent (your settings had it switched off, it fell inside your quiet hours, you were not following that game, or no device was registered). This exists so we can answer “why did I not get that notification?”, which is otherwise unanswerable after the fact. It records the delivery decision only - never the content of a notification - and is automatically deleted after three weeks.

Browser storage

The site stores a small amount of data in your browser to remember your settings between visits: theme, timezone, selected sportsbook, chosen pool format, favorite teams, whether you have seen the tutorial, the address of your current push subscription (so we can tell when your browser has silently replaced it), and the anonymous visitor ID described above. Clearing your browser's site data removes all of it.

How long we keep information

Account, league, and pick data is kept for as long as your account exists. Analytics records are kept to track traffic trends over time and are not linked to your account.

Your choices

  • Access or correction - your profile and preferences can be edited in Settings at any time.
  • Deletion - email us and we will delete your account along with your profile, picks, standings entries, league memberships, and notification subscriptions. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • Notifications - turn push off in Settings or in your browser permissions.
  • Analytics - use the Google opt-out linked above, and clear your browser storage to reset the anonymous visitor ID.

Children

Blitz Odds is not directed to children. The site displays betting odds and is intended for adults who meet the legal gambling age in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Security

Account data is stored with our hosting provider and transmitted over encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we limit what we collect in the first place and do not store payment information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
